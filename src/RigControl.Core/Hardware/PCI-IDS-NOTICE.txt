gpu-table.txt in this folder is made from pci.ids, the list of PCI IDs kept by the PCI ID Project.

Only the graphics devices of NVIDIA (10de), AMD/ATI (1002) and Intel (8086) were taken, and each name was split
into chip, name, family and architecture by tools/hardware-db/build-gpu-table.py. Nothing else was changed.
